The diglot weave method

Learn new words without leaving the story.

Diglot weave gently replaces selected words in a familiar-language text with words from the language you are learning. Context helps you infer each meaning, while a tap reveals the translation whenever you need it.

  • ✓ Target-language words are woven into meaningful context

Before your first chapter

A few things to know.

Need a hand? Contact support.

Do I need to bring my own books?

You can start with a sample story or import your own EPUB or TXT file. Use a book you have the right to read. See the import guide.

Can I import PDF or Kindle books?

The supported import formats are EPUB and TXT. PDF and Kindle formats are not supported. DRM-protected files cannot be used; choose an unprotected EPUB or plain-text file.

Which languages can I combine?

English, Spanish, French, German, and Italian are available as book and learning languages. Select two different languages, and make sure the book matches your selected reading language. Unsupported or mismatched book languages may open without word replacement.

Is this suitable for beginners?

You can choose your learning level and the amount of replacement vocabulary. Begin with familiar-language reading and a small amount of new vocabulary. This is a way to practise vocabulary alongside your other language learning.

Can I read offline?

Imported books and vocabulary analysis are processed on your device. Download any book files and required system voices first. Store purchases, subscription services, and advertising may need an internet connection.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

The free app includes ads and allows up to five books in your library at once. Pro removes ads and the five-book limit. Current subscription prices and terms are shown in the app.
