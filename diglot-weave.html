What Is Diglot Weave?

Diglot weave is a mixed-language reading approach: selected words from a language you are learning appear inside text written mainly in a language you already understand.

How a mixed-language sentence works

She opened the puerta.

The English words provide context for the Spanish word puerta, meaning “door.” You can make a guess from the sentence, then check it. A fully Spanish version would have its own grammar and sentence structure; this example is vocabulary practice inside English text.

How Linguoteka uses it

Choose the language of your book, a different language to learn, your level, and the amount of replacement vocabulary. Tap a learning word for its original meaning, pronunciation, and CEFR information. Quick Test provides a separate way to review vocabulary.

Supported book and learning languages are English, Spanish, French, German, and Italian. Read the getting-started guide for the full flow.

Keep the story readable

Start with a small amount of new vocabulary and material you enjoy. Check meanings when needed. If unfamiliar words make the story hard to follow, reduce the replacement amount rather than forcing yourself through an uncomfortable session.

What this method does not promise

Replacing individual words does not teach every grammatical pattern or capture every nuance of a translation. Vocabulary can have different meanings in different contexts. Use mixed-language reading alongside full-language reading, listening, and conversation rather than expecting it alone to deliver fluency.
